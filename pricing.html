Frequently Asked Questions

Is the initial conversation really free?

Yes. The initial conversation and visual intake are free. That is enough time to establish whether we offer the service and roughly what it will involve. Opening the computer, troubleshooting, researching parts or preparing a written diagnosis is billable hands-on work at our regular hourly rate.

How is diagnostic time billed?

Hands-on diagnostics and troubleshooting are billable. If you approve the repair, diagnostic time is included in the total labour, not added as a separate fee. For repairs reaching two labour hours or more, the assessment is fully included in the CA$200+ labour total.

What happened to Pay What You Can?

Our old open-ended sliding scale quietly turned one person into the whole safety net. It's been replaced by the Community Repair Fund, a small number of reduced-cost appointments reserved for neighbours facing genuine financial barriers. Availability is limited; please ask.

How do I pay for a repair?

You approve a quoted range before work starts, and we never exceed your authorized amount without asking. Parts are paid in advance before ordering, substantial bookings may require a 1/2hr deposit to hold the time. We accept Debit, Credit, Cash and sometimes Cheque/Direct Deposit

Where are you located, and do I need an appointment?

We're at #202 1624 56 St, Delta, BC (Tsawwassen), upstairs above BAS Appliances. All work is by appointment only. Unclaimed devices are stored for 14 days, after which storage is CA$5/day.
