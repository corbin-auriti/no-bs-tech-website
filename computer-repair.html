Computer Repair in South Delta

1 Year Standard Warranty Included

Computers are the heart of what we do here in Tsawwassen. We repair PCs, laptops and Macs for neighbours and small businesses across South Delta. The initial conversation and visual intake are free: we listen to what's going on and tell you honestly whether we're the right shop for it. Hands-on diagnostics are billable at CA$100/hour. Diagnostics That Mean Something

If a fix needs tools, disassembly or expertise, it is billable work. Hands-on diagnostics run at our CA$100/hour rate, and if you approve the repair, diagnostic time is included in the total labour. For repairs reaching two labour hours or more, the assessment is fully included in the CA$200+ labour total.

Tune-Ups & Slow Computers

Long startup times, spinning wheels, fans screaming? We track down what's actually slowing your machine down, such as software bloat, failing storage, dust or thermal issues. We fix the cause, not only the symptom.

Virus & Malware Cleanup

Pop-ups, browser hijacks, suspicious slowdowns or ransomware scares. We clean the machine properly, patch the holes that let it happen, and show you how to keep it from coming back.

Upgrades: SSD, RAM & Batteries

The single best repair for most older computers is a solid-state drive and more memory. That can make an old machine feel much faster. Parts are quoted and paid in advance, so there are no surprise parts bills later.

Laptop Screens, Keyboards & Hinges

The everyday laptop repairs: cracked or flickering screens, worn-out keyboards, loose hinges, cracked cases and charging ports that only work charging ports that only work at the right angle. Repairs are quoted before we start and warranted for a year.

Desktops, Gaming PCs & Windows

Won't power on, blue screens, random shutdowns, failed power supplies and graphics cards, gaming PC upgrades, Windows 11 setup and stubborn software. Towers, all-in-ones and small-form-factor machines are included.

Data Recovery & Backups

Dead drive? Deleted the wrong thing? We treat your data as the priority, preserving what's still there before anything else gets touched. The assessment is billed at our hourly rate, and a substantial recovery is quoted before we begin. Then we'll set up a proper backup so it never happens again.

Difficult & Board-Level Repairs

The repairs other shops decline: no-power machines, liquid damage and component-level micro-soldering under the microscope. See our Apple Mac board-level repair and console repair pages, or contact us to discuss the problem. If we can't fix it, we'll tell you who can.
