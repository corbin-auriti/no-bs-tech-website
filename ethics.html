Our Ethics and Community Commitments

Respect means honouring your time, privacy and circumstances. We want customers, families and businesses to feel welcome, heard and treated fairly.

Honesty

We explain the problem, the options and the likely cost without exaggerating the issue. For small businesses, recommendations are based on what is useful and cost-effective, not on commission. If the manufacturer can handle a device under warranty for less, we will tell you.

Humility

If we are not the right option, we will say so and help you find a better fit. Being useful sometimes means not taking the job.

Real Reviews

Our reviews come from real clients who choose to share their experience. We do not pay for reviews or offer discounts in exchange for positive feedback. We may ask after a service, but the review should reflect the client's honest experience.

High Quality Parts and Repairs

We use the best parts available to us as an independent repair business. They are not always new or original manufacturer parts. Repairs on devices from the last seven years include a one-year parts and labour warranty, which we honour.

Community Repair Fund 🧡

Community support here is a real, budgeted program, not a discount decided at the counter. Our Community Repair Fund reserves a small number of reduced-cost appointments for neighbours facing genuine financial barriers, with parts paid by the customer or a sponsor. Availability is limited, so please ask. That keeps generosity explicit, transparent and sustainable for the community and for us.
