Community Repair Fund

We reserve a small number of reduced-cost appointments for neighbours facing genuine financial barriers, people who need a working device and cannot realistically pay the full labour cost. The fund currently supports up to one hour of reduced-cost labour per month.

The fund is a real, budgeted program. It is mutual aid with clear limits, so the support can continue for the community and remain sustainable for us.

How to Ask

Just ask, mention it when you book or through our contact form. If a community organization you work with thinks a repair qualifies, they can also reach out on your behalf with the organization's name, a short description of the need, and the device. Parts are paid by the customer or a sponsor, the Fund covers labour only.

Who It's For

Neighbours facing genuine financial barriers and a real access, safety or employment need, subject to available capacity.

What It Covers

The Fund covers labour only. Parts are paid by the customer or a sponsor, and business services are not discounted. It is community support for neighbours, not a commercial discount.
