About Corbin and No BS Technologies

I grew up in Calgary with a supportive family and an early interest in building things. I earned spending money by raking leaves for neighbours and running more lemonade and baking stands than I can remember. Those small jobs taught me that good work and strong community connections go together. Around the same time, I joined the Western Canadian Robotics Society, where I learned to solder, write code and build simple circuits.

During high school, I started working at a busy computer and phone repair shop. By 2014, I was helping manage the business. In 2015, at age 18, I started this company, then called No BS Repairs, from my parents' garage while also working at Best Buy and Geek Squad.

Within a couple of years, I moved into a small subleased commercial office. When the business outgrew that space, it moved into a larger retail location. In 2021, changing family circumstances and the pandemic led me to move to Tsawwassen to be closer to family. I worked with partner companies in Calgary to transition some clients while continuing to support others remotely.

Since then, I have focused on practical computer repair and IT support built around honest conversations and clear expectations. I joined the Tsawwassen Business Improvement Association, the Ladner Business Association and the Delta Chamber of Commerce in 2021. In 2022, I became a volunteer board director with the Ladner Business Association.

Around 2023, I also became involved in local policy and advocacy work with DelPOP, focused on affordable housing, safer streets and better public transit. Running a business is ongoing work. I am still learning, improving and finding better ways to serve customers and contribute to the community.

~ Corbin Auriti
